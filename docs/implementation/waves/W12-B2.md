## 1. Uploaded-document currentness (task 1)

### 1.1 The defect, reproduced

Integration probe (`W12-INTEGRATION.md` §6/§9.1): `POST /v1/answer` with
`filters.fileIds=[<upload>]` returned `PARTIAL`, `finalizable:false`,
`reasons=[OUT_OF_DATE_SOURCE:…, NOT_FINALIZABLE]` for a one-passage answer
whose only evidence was the lawyer's own uploaded contract. Cause:
`assessCurrentness` saw no `effectiveFrom`/`decisionDate` and scored the
passage `UNKNOWN` 0.7 — below `CURRENTNESS_THRESHOLD` 0.9 — as if it were
legislation with no yürürlük. Reproduced offline in
`tests/pipeline/answerPipeline.test.ts` S7 before the fix (same fakes as the
S1–S5 demo suite).

`control-plane/src/answer/verifier.ts`

```ts
VerifiedClaim += { currentnessApplicable: boolean }
export const UPLOAD_ONLY_EVIDENCE = "UPLOAD_ONLY_EVIDENCE";      // overall reason
```

- Per claim: `currentnessBearing = supportingValid.filter(status !== NOT_APPLICABLE)`.
  `confidence.currentness = max(currentnessBearing)` when any exists (the
  **strict rule for the corpus part of a mixed answer** — an upload can never
  mask a stale statute cited by the same claim), else `max(supportingValid)`
  (= the neutral 1.0 for an upload-only claim), else 0 (no valid evidence).
  `currentnessApplicable = supportingValid.length === 0 || currentnessBearing.length > 0`.
- `finalize.ts` is unchanged in behaviour (thresholds identical; doc note
  added): the neutral score clears `CURRENTNESS_THRESHOLD`, so currentness
  never blocks an upload-only claim, and `OUT_OF_DATE_SOURCE` cannot be raised
  for it. Validation, entailment and coverage still gate it exactly as before
  (S7 tamper test).
- Overall: when every evidenced claim (≥1 validated supporting passage) is
  upload-only and the status is not ABSTAIN, `reasons += UPLOAD_ONLY_EVIDENCE`.
  A mixed answer is not marked — its corpus part carried the strict rule.
- `ENTAILMENT_BELOW_THRESHOLD:<claimId>` now follows the **finalization
  rule** (`confidence.entailment` below `ENTAILMENT_THRESHOLD`) instead of
  being recorded only on a QUALIFIED verdict. Found by the new answer-level
  eval (§3.3): a conflicted claim that also failed entailment produced
  `KESİNLEŞTİRİLEMEZ` with no reason naming the threshold. Additive (one more
  reason string); pinned by `verifierFinalizability.test.ts`.

### 3.3 What the nine PARTIAL rows are (finding, not fixed here)

Every PARTIAL row carries `ENTAILMENT_BELOW_THRESHOLD` on exactly one claim
(4 of them also `CONFLICTING_AUTHORITIES`). Probing `fx-exact-003` and
`fx-exact-005` through the real pipeline: the failing claim is in each case
the **consolidated provision claim** — `ruleDrafter` groups the fıkra
passages of one article into one claim whose text is `label: "q1"\n"q2"`,
and the lexical entailment judge scores that combined text against EACH
single passage (0.20; every single-passage claim in the same answers scores
1.00). So "one claim per provision" (2026-08-27) and the lexical judge
systematically disagree on multi-passage claims, and the answer is
KESİNLEŞTİRİLEMEZ for a mechanical reason, not a legal one. Not changed in
this lane (it moves finalization outcomes across the product; the eval now
makes it visible — that is what it was added for). Two candidate fixes for
the lane lead: judge a consolidated claim against the concatenation of its
validated passages, or judge each quoted segment against its own passage
and take the min. Before this lane the 4 conflicted rows showed NO reason
for their non-finalizability at all (§1.2, entailment reason).
